TypeMe (Browser Extension)
Version: 2.6.0
=====================================================

HOW TO LOAD IN GOOGLE CHROME, BRAVE, OR EDGE:

1. Go to:
   chrome://extensions

2. In the top-right corner, toggle ON "Developer mode".

3. Load the unpacked folder or click Refresh on the "TypeMe" card.

4. Open your target page and click the TypeMe icon in your toolbar,
   then click "▶ Launch on Current Tab".

(Press CTRL on your keyboard to toggle visibility).
